This matrix documents the adverse conditions, failure modes, boundary entries, and recovery behaviors verified in the deterministic Resilience and Failure Laboratory (`tests/resilience/`).

## Key Resilience Invariants Enforced

1. **No Data Fabrication**: When an upstream feed omits required semantic fields (e.g. title or link), the parser never fabricates fallback defaults (such as "Untitled" or placeholder URLs). The broken record is quarantined with a structured diagnostic.
2. **Snapshot Freezing**: Under any feed-level failure (transport error, 429 exhaustion, 5xx outage, or malformed XML), pre-existing last-known-good snapshots are preserved and never overwritten by empty or failed state.
3. **Data Integrity Over Telemetry**: If a failure occurs during operational logging or snapshot persistence after jobs have been written, the system does not roll back or destroy valid persisted jobs.
4. **Bounded Timing & No Storms**: `Retry-After` headers are clamped to `max_backoff_seconds` to prevent denial-of-service via huge sleep values. Concurrency caps and inter-request intervals in `RateLimiter` prevent runaway request amplification.
